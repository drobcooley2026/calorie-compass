AI connection & privacy

For AI-generated estimates, deploy the included Cloudflare Worker and enter its URL below. Never put an AI provider secret key in this page or in a public GitHub repository. Without the worker, the app uses a lightweight built-in estimate for common foods and activities.

Profile and entries are saved in this browser on this device. They are not synced between devices. AI descriptions are sent to the endpoint you configure when you request an estimate.

Calorie Compass is for general education and self-monitoring. Calorie and macro estimates can be substantially wrong, especially for restaurant meals, oils, sauces, mixed dishes, and imprecise portions. It is not intended for people under 18 or as a substitute for individualized medical or dietetic advice.
